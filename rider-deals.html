⚠️ Gear Safety Principle

Always prioritize certified personal safety gear: helmets must meet ECE 22.06 / DOT, jackets require CE Level 1 or Level 2 back and shoulder armour, and gloves must have scaphoid palm sliders. Budget accessories are great for convenience, luggage, and tools — never cheap out on impact protection.
