Privacy Policy

Nandkishor Blogs · Last updated 23 September 2026

At Nandkishor Blogs (accessible at https://www.nandkishorsharma.com), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Nandkishor Blogs and how we use it.

1. Log Files & Hosting Information

Like many websites, Nandkishor Blogs follows a standard procedure of using log files provided by our web host (Vercel). The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of this information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We use third-party advertising companies, specifically Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website.

  • Google as a Vendor: Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website and other sites across the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their browsing history.
  • Opting Out: Visitors may choose to opt out of the use of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com/. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Affiliate Disclosure & External Links

Some of the articles, gear recommendations, and tools featured on this website may contain affiliate links (including Amazon Associates). If you click through an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may receive a modest commission at absolutely zero additional cost to you. We only recommend gear and products that have been tested and used in real riding conditions.

4. Cookies & Browser Controls

Nandkishor Blogs uses basic cookies to remember user preferences (such as your Dark/Light theme selection) and to measure website performance. You can choose to disable or block cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites.

5. Children's Information

Protecting the privacy of children online is especially important. Nandkishor Blogs does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

6. Consent & Contact

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us directly via our Instagram handle @nk.rider.eats or via our Contact Us page.
